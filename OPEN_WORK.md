## 8. Lessons (scoped; not a completed survey)

Independently sourcing fitness-unit prices (*h*, *w*) may be the binding obstacle for most field tests of the law. This is a lesson from the VIS-1F pre-Pass-1 check (`theory/VIS1F_DROP_2026-11-21.md`) together with the existing sentence in `STATUS.md` that stakes *w* have never been measured validly. A classification of what the law still predicts when those prices are unknown is in `theory/PRICE_FREE_2026-11-21.md` (not a lock). The qualitative ruin-binding reversal has prior art (Stephens 1981; Caraco, Martindale & Whittam 1980; Dall & Johnstone 2002); what remains distinctive is the comparative static in the stake *w* of *S* + *w E*, with the reserve-pays-*J* / *w*²-variance mechanism. It is not a completed survey of field tests. A complementary candidate, when those field prices cannot be sourced, is tests in systems where the experimenter sets the prices (T30; `history/2026-11-21_NIGHT.md`); that path is not a lock. It does not change `CORE.md` or the law-of-adaptive-organization hypothesis sentence.

**Update, 21 Nov 2026 (W15).** The fitness-unit demand above is not needed to locate the optimum: argmin J depends only on price ratios, and prices revealed on data disjoint from the scored data are a fair test when overidentified (`theory/REVEALED_PRICES_2026-11-21.md` §1–§3). Fitness units still enter the absolute qualification band. The dataset search in that note's §9 found no public dataset that is also discriminating, so the binding constraint is now a distinctive prediction in a system with a measured world, not prices.
