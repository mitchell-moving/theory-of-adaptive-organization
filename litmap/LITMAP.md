## Map in one paragraph
Every one of GTAO's seven rules has an established ancestor, usually several in different fields that do not cite each other:
- **rule 2:** Anderson-Schooler; McNamara-Houston; Lee-Flack-Krakauer; Landes-Posner;
- **rule 1:** Denrell-March; value-of-information theory;
- **rule 7 and T1:** Reis; Alvarez-Lippi-Paciello; Sims; Stephens; Posen-Levinthal;
- **rule 3:** Kalman/Muth; Behrens; Ernst-Banks;
- **rule 5:** Rogers; Bailis;
- **rule 6:** Hebb; stigmergy; *Physarum*;
- **rule 4 / freeze:** Bateson; Waddington; Ashby; LSM trees.

The architecture is anticipated too:
- **nested levels:** Simon; hierarchy theory; Holling; Brand; Flack; Friston's nested blankets; Beer's VSM;
- **acting ahead:** Rosen; Sterling; microbial anticipation.

The closest single competitor is the SFI program of Flack and Krakauer, with Lee-Flack-Krakauer 2024 at its centre. The parts that look new are quantitative and cross-scale:
- the √N trail rule;
- the T3a threshold;
- the temperature's elasticity identity and fix-mechanism classes;
- the geometric-level count;
- a physics-to-economics exchange rate (c per nat vs kT).

They also include the *proposal to test one formula at many scales with preregistration*, which no tradition found here has done.
