**Origin.** This was seen twice post hoc: in P4f (C0.1 above ON at μ ≥ 1e-3) and in P4g (A peaks at c 0.3: 0.041 vs 0.024 at 0.01). It is now registered on fresh seeds. Known in kind: an information cost focuses attention (rational inattention; Sims 2003, doi 10.1016/S0304-3932(03)00029-1, verified in P1's crossref.out).

**Predictions.** Two one-sided Mann–Whitney tests, 20 vs 20, Holm over the two at α 0.05.
- **H1:** A at c 0.3 > A at c 0.01.
- **H2 (mechanism):** the share of compartments dominated by readers at lags outside 2–6 is lower at c 0.3.

**Status.** In-model toy; the price is a per-nat proxy; no physics claims.
