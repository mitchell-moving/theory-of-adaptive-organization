## 0. The peak, and what is carried forward

**Peak (as stated to Chad):** judgment is one process at every scale. It arises from below rather than being built in, and each level nests without new rules. Its prices must be able to fail a test, and it must be checked against observed data at more than one scale.

**Carried forward (the baby):**
- **P1 (prior art + desk check):** the sieve (Zurek–Habib–Paz 1993; Dalvit–Dziarmaga–Zurek 2005) is selection **without heredity**. The coherent state is picked by the oscillator's own symmetry, not by any cost (`../p1/desk_check_symmetry.out`).
- **P1-S, P1-S2, P1-S3 (observed, in-model):** foresight arises only where it pays and only with heredity. In P1-S3, at a world chosen so that a judge pays, selection built anticipation by action of 0.080–0.143 against the reflex reference 0.033 and the designed-judge reference 0.348. Without heredity it was ≈ 0.009, and in an unpredictable world 0.0000. It did not reach the locked bar (open miss). **So the step any "arise from below" route must supply is heredity, plus a world where the first nat pays.**
- **Side study (`../../sidechat/JUDGMENT_PEAK_2026-11-26.md`):**
  - §3 item 3: what is stationary is the action, and often not as a minimum. In the real-time path integral nothing is chosen; paths interfere. The "explore options, take the cheapest" picture is exact for imaginary-time (Euclidean) path integrals = optimal control under noise (Kappen 2005; Todorov 2009).
  - **i-gap** (line 78): quantum dynamics uses phases that cancel; optimal judgment uses weights that add. No judging process is known that produces signed or complex amplitudes.
- **Repo stance:** "It bounds a heat. It does not choose a stake, a link price, or a ruin cap." (CORE.md:23); "Temperature does not set w." (CORE.md:29).

Every published route to gravity (Jacobson, Verlinde) needs three inputs: an entropy proportional to area, a temperature, and locality. The repo has tried to get the first two from the cost and missed each time. The third has always been put in by hand.
