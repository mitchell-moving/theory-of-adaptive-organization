# P4g preregistration: a price that can fail (M5, in-model), and the high-novelty side

*Locked Friday 27 November 2026 (EST), before any test run. Calibration (calib.out, designed contest, pilot seeds): c*_designed = 0.617, with pass_frac 1.0 at c ≤ 0.5, 0.2 at 0.7, and 0 at c ≥ 1. Fresh seeds 1001–1010. One run, no retries.*

**P-cap.**
- From a zero-read start at μ 1e-3, T 6000, measure the evolved read effort E(c) for c ∈ {0.01, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 1, 2}.
- c*_evolved is the log-interpolated price where E falls through half of E(0.01).
- **PASS iff c*_evolved ∈ [0.31, 1.23]**, a factor of 2 around c*_designed. No crossing counts as a FAIL.
- A bootstrap over seeds is reported as descriptive.
- Prior signal, disclosed: P4f bracketed the evolved collapse between 0.1 and 1, so a pass is partly expected. Evolved θ is free while the designed θ was 0.3, so the two need not match.

**P-high.**
- At c 0.01, compare μ ∈ {1e-2, 3e-2, 1e-1}.
- **PASS iff** A at μ 1e-2 > A at μ 1e-1 (one-sided Mann–Whitney, p < 0.05). This is a single test, so no multiplicity correction is needed.
- It looks for the high side of a novelty band that P4f did not reach.

**Status.** In-model toy, as in P4e and P4f. The price is a linear per-nat proxy. No physics claims.
