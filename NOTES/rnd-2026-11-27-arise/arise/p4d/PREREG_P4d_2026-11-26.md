# P4d preregistration: the novelty-heredity transect

As proposed in RESULTS_AND_REVIEW_P4c. Fresh seeds 701–710, never used before. One run, no retries.*

**Setup.**
- World: crash depth τ 3, M 20 molecules, β 1.5, toxin interval P 40, c_nat 0.01, T 3000, reader **absent** at start, hedger present.
- μ ∈ {0.001, 0.003, 0.01, 0.03, 0.1}, which gives switching of about 0.004–0.13 in the P4c sweep.
- Arms at every μ:
  - ON
  - NOCUE: the drift baseline. No cue, so the "reader" core is functionally a plain core and pays no price.
  - OFF: heredity off by identity permutation.
  - RANDOM: untimed cue.
  - PRICE: c_nat 0.1.
- Reader frequency is the share of compartments with reader identity, averaged over the last 50%.

**Predictions.**
- **P1, interior optimum:** μ* = argmax over μ of S(μ) = mean reader_ON − mean reader_NOCUE is one of the three interior levels.
- **P2, the reader arises:** at μ*, the reader is first seen as identity in ≥ 2 compartments in ≥ 8/10 ON seeds.
- **P3, it spreads only where predicted:** at μ*, reader_ON beats each of hedger_ON, reader_NOCUE, reader_OFF, reader_RANDOM and reader_PRICE. One-sided Mann–Whitney (10 vs 10).

**Multiple comparisons, pre-stated.** The family is all 25 one-sided tests (5 μ × 5 comparisons), with Holm–Bonferroni at family-wise α = 0.05. P3 passes only if all 5 tests at μ* are rejected under Holm. Testing the whole family guards against picking μ* by argmax on the same data (winner's curse), which is disclosed. Per-level results are otherwise descriptive.

**Status.** In-model only, for a lumped toy chemistry, and no physics claims. Fitness is growth only (W12/W13). Known prior signals, disclosed: P4c's gate at μ 0.1 (with τ 3, M 20) showed no reader edge, and P4b's low-switching world did show one. So P1 is partly anticipated in direction. Time series are saved to series.json.
