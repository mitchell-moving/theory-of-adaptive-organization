# P4 gate v2 specification (hashed before gate v2 ran)

*Thursday 26 November 2026 (EST). This supersedes nothing: GATE_SPEC_P4 and its outputs stand as written.*

**Why there is a v2.** In gate v1 the reader lineage won 5/5 in every cell, and it also won 5/5 when the cue carried no timing information (gate_control.out). Its advantage came from making some B-core material at all (hedging), not from anticipation. So v1 could not test (ii), and the v1 design is not run.

**v2 change, the only one.** `p4b_sim.py` (SHA-256 89beae17dccdb1ce4efba815060934bc43caf4025ec61c70686c249413ee0a5d) = p4_sim.py plus a `hedge` option. With it, the A1 core (species 0 → 1, the mirror of 2 → 3) diverts the same fraction θ·Lc/P of its catalysis to B1 synthesis throughout every A phase. That gives the same mean B1 flux as the reader, untimed and unpriced. The contest becomes reader (timed, priced) vs hedger (untimed). Only the timing, the "first nat", differs.

The grid, pilot seeds 101–105, T = 1000, pass rule (≥ 0.75 in ≥ 4/5) and choice rule (largest c, then smallest δ, then smallest P) are as in v1. **Validity, built in:** at the chosen cell, the random-cue world must give reader ≥ 0.75 in ≤ 2/5, or the gate is INVALID. **If no world passes, or the gate is invalid:** report that timed reading does not pay over hedging in this class, and run no test.
