## 1. Model (fixed)
- Each agent has a body: a quantum harmonic oscillator (ħ = m = ω = 1), damping γ = 0.1, environmental position dephasing D_e = 0.05 (unread momentum diffusion), and a force f(t) shared by all agents: Ornstein–Uhlenbeck, sd σ_f = 1.5, correlation time τ_f. Gaussian (Wigner) representation, exact for linear dynamics and Gaussian states and measurements (Wiseman & Doherty 2005, doi 10.1103/PhysRevLett.94.070405). Quantum content: coherent-state births (variance 1/2 in x and p) and the imprecision–backaction product: reading at strength k gives record dy = x dt + dW/(2√k) and adds momentum diffusion k.
- Each agent has a generic 3-variable linear memory dz = Bz dt + b dy and acts with force u = g·z (|u| ≤ 5). Genome: B (9), b (3), g (3), log10 k ∈ [−4, 1], record-heredity h ∈ [0, 1]. It is not a Kalman filter and nothing in it names f.
- **Prices:** reading costs c per nat, where nats/time = Var(x | record)/(2N) for the agent's k (Duncan; steady-state Riccati of the true model), N = 1/(4k). Acting costs 0.02·u². Income 0.2 per unit time.
- **Selection (the only fitness):** death at the ruin edge |x| > 4, or when the energy budget reaches 0. Split at budget 2 (both halves get 1). Offspring: body born in the coherent state at the origin; memory z = h_parent·z_parent; genome = parent's with Gaussian mutation (0.05 on B, b, g; 0.1 on log k; 0.05 on h). Population cap 300: when full, a newborn replaces a uniformly random other agent (non-selective). **No loss term, no reward for prediction, none for heredity.**
- dt = 0.02, T = 3000, 300 founders from the random prior (B = −I + N(0, 0.2²), b, g ~ N(0, 0.5²), log k ~ U[−4, 1], h ~ U[0, 1]).

## 2. Cells (5 seeds each, seeds 1–5)
| cell | τ_f | c (per nat) | arm |
|---|---|---|---|
| P_low_on | 30 (persistent drift) | 0.02 | heredity on |
| P_low_off | 30 | 0.02 | **heredity off**: offspring genome drawn fresh from the founder prior (memory still passed with that fresh h) |
| P_low_sieve | 30 | 0.02 | **sieve only**: k = 0 (no reading), heredity on |
| U_low_on | 0.2 (fast drift) | 0.02 | heredity on |
| P_high_on | 30 | 2.0 | heredity on (zero-action limit) |

## 3. Metrics (last 20% of the run; snapshots every 1.0 time unit; agents of age ≥ 2)
- **dA_z (anticipation in the record):** R² of f(t + 2) on (1, x, p, z) minus R² on (1, x, p), pooled OLS. Information about the **future** force held in memory beyond what the body already carries.
- dA_u: the same with u in place of z (secondary, reported only).
- **ruin hazard:** ruin deaths per agent-time. budget hazard reported.
- **h_mean:** time-average of the population mean of h. **logk_median:** time-average of the population median of log10 k.

## 4. Predictions and thresholds (paired by seed; "≥ 4/5" means at least 4 of the 5 seeds)
- **H-A anticipation emerges:** P_low_on dA_z ≥ 0.05 in ≥ 4/5.
- **H-B it needs heredity:** dA_z(P_low_on) − dA_z(P_low_off) ≥ 0.03 in ≥ 4/5.
- **H-C the judge beats the sieve at equal prices:** ruin hazard(P_low_on) ≤ 0.7 × ruin hazard(P_low_sieve) in ≥ 4/5.
- **H-D the heredity-off null loses:** ruin hazard(P_low_off) ≥ 1.3 × ruin hazard(P_low_on) in ≥ 4/5.
- **H-E record heredity follows drift persistence (W10 / Kalman condition):** h_mean(P_low_on) − h_mean(U_low_on) ≥ 0.15 in ≥ 4/5.
- **H-F zero-action limit (sieve recovered at high price):** P_high_on logk_median ≤ −3 and |dA_z| < 0.05 in ≥ 4/5.
- Extinction in a seed counts as a fail for every prediction using that seed's cell.

**Scoring.** Primary (Qe, emergence): **HIT** iff H-A, H-B, H-C and H-D all pass. Otherwise **open miss**, logged with sizes. H-E and H-F are scored separately and do not rescue or sink the primary. No threshold, metric, cell or seed is changed after the lock; any extra analysis is labeled post hoc.

## 5. What each outcome would mean (stated before the run)
- HIT: in this model, a record that anticipates the world arises by survival selection alone, needs heredity, and beats a sieve at the same prices. Judgment emerges from a sieve start under the theory's named conditions. Observed in-model; not evidence about nature.
- H-A pass but H-B fail: anticipation arises without heredity, a sieve-style (survivorship) story suffices, which counts against the heredity clause (N28/SE4 iii) in this model.
- H-A fail: survival-only selection did not build anticipation here; the judge does not emerge at this boundary under these prices.
- H-E fail: record heredity is not selected by drift persistence; the W10 / Kalman condition does not show up as an evolved trait.
