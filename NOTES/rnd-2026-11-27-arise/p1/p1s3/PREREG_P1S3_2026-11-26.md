## 0. Design choice that limits a pass (stated before the run)
The world was **chosen by a gate** so that a designed judge beats both a sieve and a reflex controller in head-to-head selection. **Tuning the world until a judge can win is a design choice.** A pass would show only that survival selection, starting from random linear controllers, **can find** anticipation **where it already pays**. It would not show that judges pay in general, that this world is typical, or anything about nature. A miss would be stronger evidence than in P1-S/S2, because the reward for judging is now known to be there.

## 1. Fixes applied
- **Fix 1 (gate):** all 24 grid points were run on pilot seeds 101–105 (`gate.out`); 11 passed. The rule picked the least judge-friendly passing world: **σ_f = 1.0, c = 0.02 per nat, ruin edge x_r = 3**, design k = 0.1. There, a designed judge went to frequency 1.0 against the sieve (5/5) and against the reflex (5/5), with a median ruin share of deaths of 0.632.
- **Fix 2 (selection strength):** no random culls at a cap. Shared income R = 50 is split among the living, with basal upkeep m = 0.2 and a small random death μ = 0.005. Deaths come from ruin, starvation and μ.
- **Fix 3:** one force path per seed, generated from its own random stream and identical in every arm.
- **Fix 4:** record heredity is per memory variable (h_1, h_2, h_3 ∈ [0, 1], evolvable). The offspring body is born fresh at the origin, so selection can pass on world-tracking memory without passing on stale body state.
- **Fix 6:** anticipation is measured by **action** (u has the same units for every agent, so pooling is valid): dA_u = R²(f(t+2) ~ 1, x, p, u) − R²(f(t+2) ~ 1, x, p).
- **Fix 7:** a full-length pilot (T = 30000, seed 106) printed extinction only: P_on survived, P_off survived, **P_sieve went extinct at t = 595**, U_on survived, High_on survived. Designed references (fixed monocultures, seed 106): **judge dA_u = 0.348; reflex dA_u = 0.033**; a sieve monoculture went extinct at t = 62.

## 2. Cells (world as chosen; T = 30000; fresh test seeds 301–305)
P_on (heredity on), P_off (offspring genome drawn fresh from the founder prior), P_sieve (no reading), U_on (τ_f = 0.2, fast drift), High_on (c = 0.2, ten times the price). 250 founders from the random prior; mutation as in P1-S plus 0.05 on each h_j.

## 4. Predictions and thresholds ("≥ 4/5" = at least 4 of the 5 seeds; extinction fails every prediction that needs that cell's metrics)
- **H-A, anticipation emerges (primary):** P_on dA_u ≥ 0.19 (the reflex reference plus half the gap to the designed judge: 0.033 + 0.5 × 0.315) in ≥ 4/5.
- **H-B, it needs heredity (primary):** dA_u(P_on) − dA_u(P_off) ≥ 0.10 in ≥ 4/5.
- **H-D, the heredity-off null loses (primary):** P_off extinct, or ruin hazard(P_off)/ruin hazard(P_on) ≥ 1.3, in ≥ 4/5.
- **H-C, reading beats no reading:** P_on not extinct, and P_sieve extinct or its ruin hazard ≥ 1/0.7 of P_on's, in ≥ 4/5. **Flagged before the run: the gate and pilot make this near-certain. It carries little information and is not part of the primary.**
- **H-E, record heredity follows drift persistence:** h_max(P_on) − h_max(U_on) ≥ 0.10 in ≥ 4/5.
- **H-F, price pushes reading down:** median log k(High_on) ≤ median log k(P_on) − 0.5 in ≥ 4/5.

**Primary (emergence) HIT iff H-A, H-B and H-D all pass**; otherwise **open miss**, logged with sizes. H-C, H-E and H-F are scored separately. No changes to cells, metrics, thresholds or seeds after the lock; anything else is labeled post hoc.
