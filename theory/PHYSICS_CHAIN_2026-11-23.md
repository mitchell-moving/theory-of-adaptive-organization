## 1. The GTAO method on this chain

The method is the same at every link (`CORE.md:9, 48, 64`):

1. **Write the cost.** In the physical class (`CORE.md:27, 66`), the long-run rate is \(J = \dot Q_M + w\,\mathbb{E}[\ell]\) subject to \(P(\mathrm{ruin})\le\varepsilon_{\mathrm{ruin}}\) (memory-bath heat plus stake times expected decision loss; separate from `THEORY.md` §3's five-term sum, `CORE.md:31-44`, [`OPEN_WORK.md:91`](https://github.com/Chad-Mitchell/theory-of-adaptive-organization/blob/1eed183f3888e8e3eead5956bbf1e8cb4233510a/OPEN_WORK.md#L91)). When the memory acts on the world (`theory/FEEDBACK_FLOOR_2026-11-23.md:56`), the world-bath actuation heat \(\dot Q_X\) enters as \(J = \dot Q_M + \dot Q_X + w\,\varepsilon_{\mathrm{task}}\). Temperature does not fix the stake \(w\) (`CORE.md:29`).
2. **Find the minimum.** Under a thermodynamic floor \(\dot Q_M \ge kT\,\dot I_M \ge kT\,\Phi(d)\) (`CORE.md:68-70`), minimize \(kT\,\Phi(d) + w\,d\) over attainable distortion \(d\), or minimize total heat plus stake in the feedback class (`theory/FEEDBACK_FLOOR_2026-11-23.md:33-35`).
3. **Read off the design.** Freeze static degrees of freedom at zero ongoing heat (`CORE.md:78`), route boundary learning flux only to changing degrees of freedom that the decision uses (`theory/AREA_LAW_PROBE_2026-11-23.md:127`), use fast or aligned memories where tracking pays (`CORE.md:74, 76`), and respect boundary channel capacity (`theory/AREA_LAW_PROBE_2026-11-23.md:107-109`).
4. **State scope and open measurement.** In-model bounds hold only inside their named classes (`CORE.md:11, 60`). Whether real systems sit near the minimum (`CORE.md:11, 13, 138`), non-MSE / feedback extensions of T49 (`theory/NON_GAUSSIAN_FLOOR_2026-11-23.md:28`), the repair heat of self-correcting media and quantum records in 3D (after T63), and gravitational inputs (`theory/AREA_LAW_PROBE_2026-11-23.md:145`) remain open or empirical.

## 2. Level 1 — PROVED (in-model): the heat floor across classes

- **Thermodynamic floor \(\dot Q_M \ge kT\,\dot I_M\) (`CORE.md:70`).** **KNOWN** for bipartite Markov jumps (Horowitz and Esposito 2014, doi `10.1103/PhysRevX.4.031015`) and **PROVED (in-model)** for additive-noise diffusions (`EQ-heat`). The bound prices the *net* learning rate of memory transitions, not unsigned jump traffic.
  - *What would break it:* Non-bipartite transitions, non-local-detailed-balance protocols, non-Einstein noise, or coupling to an auxiliary bath that pays the dissipation externally (`CORE.md:70`, `theory/GENERAL_FLOOR_ATTEMPT_2026-11-23.md:13, 55`, `theory/FREEZE_OR_HOLD_FLOOR_2026-11-23.md:17`).
  - *Next proof:* Generalize \(\Phi(d)\) via quantitative mixing or amplification inequalities (`theory/GENERAL_FLOOR_ATTEMPT_2026-11-23.md:15`).
- **Continuous A6 coordinate (T48, `theory/CONTINUOUS_A6_2026-11-23.md:13-28`, [`OPEN_WORK.md:99`](https://github.com/Chad-Mitchell/theory-of-adaptive-organization/blob/1eed183f3888e8e3eead5956bbf1e8cb4233510a/OPEN_WORK.md#L99)).** **KNOWN** + **PROVED (in-model)** packaging: on standard Borel spaces with finite mutual information, A6's memory stock \(I(M_t;X^t)\) is the Gelfand–Yaglom–Dobrushin supremum of discrete mutual informations, equal to \(D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_{X,Y}\|P_X\otimes P_Y)\) and to differential mutual information when densities exist. Theorem R1 is unchanged; the gross stock \(I(M_t;X^t)\) stays distinct from the net learning rate \(I_{\mathrm{alt}}\) (`:28`).
  - *What would break it:* Infinite mutual information (`:26`), where R1 is not applied, or conflating differential entropy with finite-law Aczél–Forte–Ng uniqueness (`:26`).
  - *Next proof:* None for A6 (non-Gaussian exogenous MSE floors are in T49 below); what stays outside A6 is infinite mutual information (`:26`).
- **Drifting continuous worlds (T58, `theory/DRIFT_HEAT_LIFT_2026-11-23.md:16-30, 78-94`, [`OPEN_WORK.md:109`](https://github.com/Chad-Mitchell/theory-of-adaptive-organization/blob/1eed183f3888e8e3eead5956bbf1e8cb4233510a/OPEN_WORK.md#L109)).** **PROVED (in-model)** for an overdamped Einstein spring tracking a random walk: \(\dot I_M = \sigma^2/(2\varepsilon)\); a Kalman–Bucy-matched spring (sensory capacity \(1\)) pays \(2kT\,\bar r\) (matching Sandberg et al. 2014, doi `10.1103/PhysRevE.90.042119`, and Hartich et al. 2016, doi `10.1103/PhysRevE.93.022116`), while a fast noisier spring approaches the factor-one infimum \(kT\,\sigma^2/(2\varepsilon)\).
  - *What would break it:* Non-linear dynamics, discrete-time updates, or restoring/unstable drifts where the filter copy is work rather than bath heat (`theory/DRIFT_HEAT_LIFT_2026-11-23.md:105, 123-124`).
  - *Next proof:* General capacity-one factor-two accounting outside linear Einstein springs (`CORE.md:82`, `theory/DRIFT_HEAT_LIFT_2026-11-23.md:125`).
- **Frozen vs. changing two-state worlds (T59, `theory/FREEZE_OR_HOLD_FLOOR_2026-11-23.md:15-27, 76-85, 144-146`, [`OPEN_WORK.md:110`](https://github.com/Chad-Mitchell/theory-of-adaptive-organization/blob/1eed183f3888e8e3eead5956bbf1e8cb4233510a/OPEN_WORK.md#L110)).** **PROVED (in-model)** for a telegraph world and leaky two-state memory under local detailed balance: leak rate \(\Gamma\) cancels in the net flow, giving \(\dot Q_M \ge kT\,r\,L(\varepsilon)\). At \(r=0\) (frozen world), a static bias is at equilibrium with zero ongoing heat at a finite Boltzmann error; a high barrier buys lifetime \(\sim 1/\Gamma\), not a steady heat floor.
  - *What would break it:* An irreversible (non-LDB) refresh protocol that enforces a per-reset Landauer cost (`:17, 172`), which is the entry point for T63.
  - *Next proof:* Done as T63 (`theory/RECORD_REPAIR_FLOOR_2026-11-23.md`, PR #240): the repair floor for a record of a vanished bit is **PROVED (in-model, class RF)**.
- **Connected finite-state worlds (T60, `theory/GENERAL_FLOOR_FINITE_2026-11-23.md:18-29, 89`, [`OPEN_WORK.md:111`](https://github.com/Chad-Mitchell/theory-of-adaptive-organization/blob/1eed183f3888e8e3eead5956bbf1e8cb4233510a/OPEN_WORK.md#L111)).** **PROVED (in-model)** for exogenous steady Markov-jump worlds: \(I_{\mathrm{alt}} = \sum_{x\neq x'}\pi(x)W_X(x\to x')D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p(M\mid x)\,\|\,p(M\mid x'))\). Strongly connected occupied flux graphs force \(I_{\mathrm{alt}}>0\) whenever Bayes error beats chance (one direction for \(\ge 3\) states), giving the world-only Path-Pinsker bound \((\varepsilon_0-\varepsilon)^2/K_X\), pairwise \(L\) bound, and telegraph bound \(r\,L(\varepsilon)\) for any finite memory. Disconnected recurrent classes still allow zero ongoing heat by holding a class label (`:21`).

- **Feedback / acting worlds (T61, `theory/FEEDBACK_FLOOR_2026-11-23.md:15-37, 56`, [`OPEN_WORK.md:112`](https://github.com/Chad-Mitchell/theory-of-adaptive-organization/blob/1eed183f3888e8e3eead5956bbf1e8cb4233510a/OPEN_WORK.md#L112)).** **PROVED (in-model)** in the named classes: `JP-heat` and `EQ-heat` still hold under feedback (\(I_{\mathrm{alt}}=-I^X\)), but the T60 exogenous identity and any design-independent positive floor on \(\dot Q_M\) alone fail (`:17`).   - *Next proof:* Dual control and the closed-form infimum of total heat \(\dot Q_M+\dot Q_X\) at fixed variance (`theory/FEEDBACK_FLOOR_2026-11-23.md:35`, `CORE.md:80`).

## 4. Level 3 — OPEN chain to holography and gravity

Each step below separates what is proved in-model from the open conjectures and external physical inputs needed to travel from the cost of judgment to spacetime geometry.

1. **Link 3a — Tracked boundary flux scales with area (PROVED in-model, given a channel packing input; T62, T41, T42, T44).**
   In class AL (`theory/AREA_LAW_PROBE_2026-11-23.md:103, 107-109`, [`OPEN_WORK.md:113`](https://github.com/Chad-Mitchell/theory-of-adaptive-organization/blob/1eed183f3888e8e3eead5956bbf1e8cb4233510a/OPEN_WORK.md#L113)), \(\dot I_M \le C_B \le \varphi A\): sustainable tracked learning rate is capped by boundary channel capacity \(C_B\) and scales at most with boundary area \(A\) given a per-area capacity density \(\varphi\). This builds on `T41` (`theory/HOLOGRAPHIC_2026-11-22.md:40, 68, 96`, [`OPEN_WORK.md:92`](https://github.com/Chad-Mitchell/theory-of-adaptive-organization/blob/1eed183f3888e8e3eead5956bbf1e8cb4233510a/OPEN_WORK.md#L92): the floor is the interface learning rate `H-iface`, bulk per-site tracking is volume `H-volume`, surface observation removes at most \(A/\alpha\) `H-surface`) and `T44` (`theory/BOUNDARY_SUFFICIENCY_2026-11-22.md:39, 45, 105`, [`OPEN_WORK.md:95`](https://github.com/Chad-Mitchell/theory-of-adaptive-organization/blob/1eed183f3888e8e3eead5956bbf1e8cb4233510a/OPEN_WORK.md#L95): the Markov blanket screens exterior decisions `BS-screen`/`BS-loss`, not interior ones `BS-interior`). Shared storage (`T42`, `theory/HOLOGRAPHIC_MEMORY_2026-11-22.md:46, 52`, [`OPEN_WORK.md:93`](https://github.com/Chad-Mitchell/theory-of-adaptive-organization/blob/1eed183f3888e8e3eead5956bbf1e8cb4233510a/OPEN_WORK.md#L93)) compares sum-readout vs. per-item readout and is not Bekenstein–Hawking entropy. Crucially, `AL-stock-free` (`theory/AREA_LAW_PROBE_2026-11-23.md:11, 111`) proves that equilibrium static-bias records have zero ongoing heat and still pack by volume even after a bounded-rate write (`AL-write`, `:115`), while tracking a volumetric changing world remains a volume cost (`AL-volume-track`, `:117-125`).
   - *What would break Link 3a:* Volume-distributed read channels bypassing the boundary, or unbounded channel packing density \(\varphi\).
   - *Next proof:* Extend class AL beyond bipartite Markov/Langevin cuts to quantum channels (`theory/AREA_LAW_PROBE_2026-11-23.md:145`).

2. **Link 3b — Leaky-record repair heat exits through the surface (PROVED in-model only for media that cannot self-correct, given a shed-flux input; evaded by classical self-correcting media in 2D and 3D; T63).**
   T63 (`theory/RECORD_REPAIR_FLOOR_2026-11-23.md`, PR #240, independently reviewed) proves, in class RF, that keeping a record of a vanished bit at error \(\varepsilon\) in carriers that each leak at rate at least \(\Gamma\) dissipates at least \(4kT\Gamma(\ln 2-h(\varepsilon))\) per record per unit time over long horizons, for any code, decoder and corrector modeled inside the joint Markov process (one carrier: exactly \(kT\Gamma L(\varepsilon)\), a value also in Zhao 2026). With a stated input that the region sheds at most \(q\) per unit boundary area, the number of records kept is at most \(qA/(kT\Gamma g(\varepsilon))\) (RF-cap). The area comes from the input \(q\), not from the cost; the cap has no Planck coefficient and sits about 38 orders of magnitude below the holographic density at the 1 eV example. The lower bound is \(kT\) times the medium's modified log-Sobolev constant, at most twice its spectral gap, and classical cooperative media below their ordering temperature in \(d=2,3\) (Thomas 1989) hold records passively at zero ongoing heat with block sizes polylogarithmic in the horizon, so stock returns to volume scaling. Quantum records: no 2D stabilizer self-correction, partial in 3D, full in 4D; the 3D quantum case is **OPEN**.
   - *What broke, and what stands:* The cap does not hold for all records, because classical self-correction evades it in 2D and 3D. It stands only for media that cannot self-correct, given \(q\).
   - *Next proof:* An input that limits passive cooperative media (for example, a gravitational bound on the energy a region can hold, or a horizon temperature below which no barrier is safe), stated explicitly and tested against the self-correction loophole; plus an upper bound on the repair heat of self-correcting blocks (T63 §9).

3. **Link 3c — Horizon-temperature input fixes the coefficient without supplying \(G\) (CONJECTURE).**
   The cost of judgment contains neither \(G\), \(\hbar\), nor \(c\) (`theory/AREA_LAW_PROBE_2026-11-23.md:17, 135`). Supplying one external physical input — that a horizon sheds heat at the Unruh or Hawking temperature (Unruh 1976, doi `10.1103/PhysRevD.14.870`; Hawking 1975, doi `10.1007/BF02345020`; `theory/AREA_LAW_PROBE_2026-11-23.md:39, 43, 145`) — could fix the temperature and shed-flux scale in a T63-type cap without the cost itself generating \(G\) or Planck units. Until then, `theory/SCALE_ENDS.md:180-188, 192, 247, 254` and `theory/FREEZE_OR_HOLD_FLOOR_2026-11-23.md:182-188` keep black holes as the extreme of the frozen class with the area law and Page curve as **inputs**, not outputs.
   - *What would break Link 3c:* If the stock cap comes entirely from the gravitational or causal input (Bekenstein 1973, doi `10.1103/PhysRevD.7.2333`; Bousso 2002, doi `10.1103/RevModPhys.74.825`; Casini 2008, doi `10.1088/0264-9381/25/20/205021`) while the cost of judgment adds only a relabeling (`theory/AREA_LAW_PROBE_2026-11-23.md:145`), or if no cap survives self-correction (T63 yields the volume-repair-to-surface-shedding cap only for media that cannot self-correct).
   - *Next proof (queued):* **After T63, run a horizon-temperature input test** (`theory/AREA_LAW_PROBE_2026-11-23.md:145`) stating the Unruh/Hawking temperature and shed-flux input explicitly and testing, for media that cannot self-correct and for whatever input limits passive cooperative media, whether T63's cap recovers \(S\propto A\) (and whether any prefactor matches) without circularly assuming \(S\le A/(4\ell_p^2)\).

4. **Link 3d — From an area law plus thermodynamics to Einstein's equations (KNOWN, prior art — Jacobson 1995).**
   Jacobson (1995, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* **75**:1260–1263, doi `10.1103/PhysRevLett.75.1260`, verified on Crossref) shows that local Rindler horizons, the Unruh temperature, the Clausius relation \(\delta Q = T\,dS\), and an area-proportional entropy \(S \propto A\) yield the Einstein equation as an equation of state (`theory/AREA_LAW_PROBE_2026-11-23.md:17, 51, 137`).
