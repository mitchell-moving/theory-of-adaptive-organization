# IIT path: cost-based irreducibility, IIT's limits, and a consciousness conjecture

*Monday 23 November 2026 (America/New_York). An exploration path, not a theorem and not a change to `THEORY.md`, `theory/BOOKENDS.md`, or the hypothesis sentence. This page writes the cost of a shared tracking layer, finds the cheapest-partition surplus \(I_J\), and marks where that object bears on integrated information theory (IIT) and on consciousness. Predictions below are candidates, not preregistered. No new computation. Open-work row T56. Adapted from a same-day research memo written against main `801d4ff` and revised after an independent review; this is the public, shortened form.*

Labels follow the repo: **PROVED (in-model)** is a result inside a named class. **KNOWN** is published and credited, only for what that source states. **ELEMENTARY** is a direct consequence of a proved cost, not a new named theorem. **CONJECTURE** is a named bet, not a result. **INTERPRETATION** maps a proved result onto a real system; the mapping is not proved. **ANALOGY** is a shared template, not a derivation. **SPECULATION** is not tested and is not derived. **IMPOSTER** is a claim that sounds as if it follows from GTAO but does not. **OPEN / UNTESTED** means not measured.

The law of adaptive organization (hypothesis): strongly selected systems minimize the cost of judgment.

---

## 1. The use

This is a path to explore, not a result to adopt.

Minimizing the cost of judgment over a group of trackers yields a real irreducibility number, \(I_J\): the extra expected tracking cost of the cheapest partition of a shared layer. In the already-proved pooling class, the comparison against splitting everyone apart is the familiar CT14 pooling gain; \(I_J\) itself is that same cost compared across every cut. It has the same min-over-cuts shape as IIT's system integrated information, and it is not Tononi's \(\Phi\).

The theory is not neutral on consciousness. The cost already says which coalitions should form, last, overlap, or split, and it already disagrees with IIT where the two partition rules come apart: IIT's measure is stake-free and world-optional; GTAO's surplus is a cost of tracking a driver; IIT's exclusion rule forbids overlapping complexes, while GTAO allows a part to join every paying shared layer when drivers are independent. Those contrasts are in the costs as written. They become a contradiction with exclusion only if a shared layer is identified with a complex.

What the cost does not contain is a variable for experience. Identifying a currently paying shared layer with a conscious episode is a **CONJECTURE**, not a theorem. Any claim that the present theorems show a system is conscious remains an **IMPOSTER**, as `theory/BOOKENDS.md` already says (lines 74, 254, 407). This page does not rewrite that bookends line. It records the cost's partition object, the comparison with IIT, and the labeled conjecture as a path that may relate to consciousness and may help locate IIT's limits.

## 2. The method: write the cost, find the minimum, read off the design

Take the architecture class \(\mathcal{A}_1\) of CT14–CT16 (`THEORY.md` B.4), already proved. \(N\) members each track \(x_i=g+u_i\), with \(g\) and the \(u_i\) independent, the same drift exponent, levels \(q_g\) and \(q_u\), and \(\rho=q_g/q_u\). A sensing technology has minimized cost \(\varphi_{\mathrm{cost}}(w,q)=K\,p^{1-a}w^a q^b\) (the repo's \(\varphi(w,q)\); the subscript keeps it off IIT's \(\varphi_s\)). Stakes are \(w\) each.

For a candidate grouping \(S\) and a partition \(\pi\) of \(S\) into blocks, compare a *joint* design (one shared tracker of the common driver, plus residual local trackers) with a *cut* design (a shared tracker inside each block, plus residuals):

\[
J(S,\mathrm{joint})=\varphi_{\mathrm{cost}}(W_S,q_g)+\sum_{i\in S}\varphi_{\mathrm{cost}}(w_i,q_{u_i})+h_S,
\]

\[
J(S,\pi)=\sum_{B\in\pi}\Big(\varphi_{\mathrm{cost}}(W_B,q_g^{(B)})+\sum_{i\in B}\varphi_{\mathrm{cost}}(w_i,q_{u_i})+h_B\Big).
\]

Here \(q_g^{(B)}\) is the drift level of the component common inside block \(B\). For a singleton that component is \(x_i\) itself, at level \(q_g+q_u\), with no residual — CT14's flat design (one tracker of \(x_i\) per member). A per-member split into a \(g\)-tracker plus a residual would be strictly worse in this class (\(0<b<1\)) and is not the comparison that yields \(G(N)\).

The **surplus of the shared signal across the cut** is the reduction in expected tracking cost from not cutting along \(\pi\):

\[
\Delta J(S;\pi)\;:=\;J(S,\pi)-J(S,\mathrm{joint}).
\]

The **minimum-surplus partition** and the **cost-based irreducibility** are

\[
\pi^*(S)=\underset{\pi\neq\{S\}}{\arg\min}\,\Delta J(S;\pi),\qquad I_J(S)\;:=\;\min_{\pi\neq\{S\}}\Delta J(S;\pi).
\]

A shared layer for \(S\) beats the weakest cut, after upkeep, iff \(I_J(S)>0\). Against the *flat* cut (all singletons) this is exactly CT14's gain \(G(N)=N[(1+\rho)^b-1]-N^a\rho^b-h'\), which crosses zero once, so the level beats flat iff \(N>N^*\). At \(h=0\), \(N^*=\big[\rho^b/((1+\rho)^b-1)\big]^{1/(1-a)}\); for square-root technology, \(N^*=(\sqrt{1+\rho}+1)^2/\rho\). CT16 adds a telling price: even when pooling would pay, dissemination can still forbid a central copy, iff \(d/c\) is too large. CT19 adds a latency veto: a cut whose delay exceeds \(L_{\max}=v(D^*/q)^{1/(\alpha-1)}\) cannot be closed by one tracker, whatever the pooling gain.

**Status.** The flat-cut case is **PROVED (in-model)** (CT14, CT16, CT19). The min-over-all-partitions form \(I_J\) is **ELEMENTARY** in the same class: it is the same cost, compared on a larger set of feasible designs, not a new mechanism and not a theorem already named in the repo. Intermediate partitions can matter (two weakly coupled blocks should be cut at the bridge). CT14 already warns that \(N>N^*\) is *sufficient* for *some* shared design to beat flat, not a unique cutoff, and that a joint design outside \(\mathcal{A}_1\) can only do better.

The swarm stay condition sits on a different axis of the same cost. For a part already facing a whole, some schedule beats leaving iff \(\lambda s_i c < w_i\Phi_{\mathrm{CT9}}\) with \(\Phi_{\mathrm{CT9}}=\int_0^\infty(r_\infty-r(u))\,du\) (CT9; **PROVED (in-model)**; `theory/SWARM_RULE_2026-11-23.md`). \(\Phi_{\mathrm{CT9}}\) is the worth of the whole's *signal to that part*, not the irreducibility of the part's cause–effect power over itself. Sharing the letter \(\Phi\) with IIT is typographic collision, not a bridge. The repo already uses \(\Phi\) for CT9, \(\varphi(w,q)\) for the sensing cost, and \(\Phi(z)\) for the normal cdf; IIT's \(\varphi_s\) and structure-\(\Phi\) are further uses.

## 3. What \(I_J\) is not

IIT 4.0's system integrated information \(\varphi_s\) is a stake-free, world-optional loss of intrinsic cause–effect information across a *normalized* minimum partition (Albantakis, Barbosa, Findlay, Grasso et al. 2023; the 3.0 statement is Oizumi, Albantakis and Tononi 2014; earlier statements are Tononi 2004 and 2008). After the minimum information partition is chosen, \(\varphi_s\) is the unnormalized loss. IIT has no cost, no stakes, and no required driver in the *definition* of a complex.

\(I_J\) and \(\varphi_s\) share a min-over-cuts template and are not the same quantity. The integrand differs: expected tracking cost in upkeep plus stake times loss, versus intrinsic bits under interventional cuts. They are not identified by the theory as it stands, and an identity \(I_J=f(\varphi_s)\) for some monotone \(f\) would need a stack of extra assumptions that undo the theory (take the world to be the system itself, drop or flatten costs and stakes, impose exclusion by hand, and ignore latency). In that zero-cost limit the cost has nothing left to say that IIT did not already say. A shared template is an **ANALOGY**, not a derivation; graph min-cuts and several information decompositions use the same kind of object.

Two further contrasts follow from the costs as written, without any consciousness claim:

1. **Overlapping layers versus exclusion.** If drivers are independent and costs add, a part joins every shared layer whose test it passes (`theory/FLUID_LAYERS_2026-11-21.md`; **PROVED (in-model)**, elementary). IIT 4.0's exclusion postulate says overlapping candidate substrates compete and only the maximum-\(\varphi_s\) set exists as a complex; non-overlapping complexes may coexist (Albantakis et al. 2023; **KNOWN**, as IIT's rule). Those are different partition rules. Identifying "shared layer" with "complex" contradicts exclusion whenever two independent drivers both pay — which the repo already treats as the normal case. The contradiction holds only under that identification.
2. **Federation is forced by latency, not by a \(\varphi_s\) contest.** Past \(L_{\max}\) a system must federate (CT19; **PROVED (in-model)**). IIT has no latency-error budget in the definition of a complex. Temporal grain, and exclusion over timescales, is a nearby IIT knob; it is not a substitute for a delay-versus-error bound.
3. **An idle high-\(\varphi_s\) grid is not a selected whole.** A substrate with large \(\varphi_s\) that tracks no needed shared driver is not kept by the cost (CT9, CT14; Still, Sivak, Bell and Crooks 2012, already `theory/THEOREMS.md` T6). Tononi accepted that a grid can be highly conscious, in the 2014 exchange with Aaronson (**KNOWN**, as IIT's implication in that exchange; the posts have no Crossref DOI and none is claimed here). This page does not recompute IIT \(\varphi_s\) on those designs.

## 4. Where it bears on consciousness, without overclaiming

A brain (or a coalition of cells, or an agent) can be read as a swarm of parts, each minimizing its own \(J_i\), facing shared and private drivers, paying to read, staying while \(\lambda s c < w\Phi_{\mathrm{CT9}}\), joining a shared layer when \(N>N^*\), leaving or federating when that fails or when latency exceeds \(L_{\max}\), and re-forming when a grouping drifts outside the CL19 band. That reading is an **INTERPRETATION** of proved costs. It yields measurable claims about which coalitions should form, last, overlap, or split. It still does not mention experience.

To reach consciousness one must add an identification, for example: *a conscious episode is (or tracks) a currently paying shared layer.* That step is a **CONJECTURE**. It is allowed, if labeled. It is not entailed. It immediately produces disagreements with IIT that can be tested, which is the content of §5. Until \(N,\rho,\lambda,c,w,\Phi_{\mathrm{CT9}}\) (or operational stand-ins) are measured in a qualifying nervous system, a "GTAO explains consciousness" or "GTAO shows this system is conscious" claim remains an **IMPOSTER**.

A cleaner empirical programme, which does not require that identification, is the **weak** one distinguished by Mediano, Rosas, Bor, Seth and Barrett 2022 (**KNOWN** framing): ask whether \(I_J\), pooling onset, or the stay line tracks the same variance as the perturbational complexity index (PCI; Casali, Gosseries, Rosanova, Boly et al. 2013) or a practical \(\Phi\)-like time-series measure, in anesthesia, sleep, and dual-task data. PCI is IIT-*inspired*, not a computation of \(\varphi_s\). That is a correlation test, preregisterable, and it can miss without anyone having to decide what experience *is*.

Causal emergence work from the IIT group is already in the repo as a test of whether a GTAO *level* is real, not of experience: Hoel, Albantakis and Tononi 2013, with Rosas et al. 2020, at `litmap/LITMAP.md` line 221 (EXTENDS) and `litmap/PRIOR_ART.md` line 31. This page does not rediscover that citation. The thermodynamic floor "does not explain consciousness" (`core/bridge_2026-11-21/BRIDGE.md` line 62); that sentence stands.

## 5. Candidate predictions (not preregistered)

All four numbered rows, and the weak-programme candidate that follows them, are dated **23 November 2026**. None is locked. None is a field test already run. A miss stays open for the stated class, not a test of the hypothesis sentence.

**C-IIT-1. Overlapping paying layers, against IIT exclusion.** (**CONJECTURE** on the experience identification; the layering half is **PROVED (in-model)**.) If two independent drivers each pass CT14 and costs add, a part can belong to both shared layers at once. If a conscious episode is identified with a currently paying shared layer, overlapping, simultaneously "on" coalitions are allowed and expected. IIT 4.0 exclusion forbids overlapping complexes. A miss, in a THEORY §3–qualifying class with operational stand-ins for the pooling test: the two coalitions never overlap when both contents are reported, or whenever they overlap, one is systematically dropped in the way exclusion requires, even though both pooling tests pass. **UNTESTED.**

**C-IIT-2. Idle high-\(\varphi_s\) structure is not a selected whole.** (**CONJECTURE** on experience; the cost half is **PROVED (in-model)**.) A substrate with large IIT \(\varphi_s\) that tracks no needed shared driver is not a selected whole, and under the experience identification it is not a conscious whole. A miss: a preregistered system whose shared-layer and stay tests all fail, but whose \(\varphi_s\) (or a pre-registered practical proxy that the protocol does not itself treat as the definition of consciousness) is high, nonetheless meets a pre-registered independent criterion of a conscious whole; or the converse, a system whose stay and pooling tests all pass, with \(\varphi_s\) near zero, meets that same criterion. Using PCI as both the IIT-side proxy and the consciousness criterion would not discriminate. **UNTESTED.**

**C-IIT-3. Latency veto versus max-\(\varphi_s\) unity.** (**INTERPRETATION** of a **PROVED (in-model)** bound; **CONJECTURE** if applied to experience.) Two regions whose communication delay exceeds \(L_{\max}\) for the drift they would jointly track will federate (CT19). IIT would still score a single complex if the joint substrate maximized \(\varphi_s\). A miss, in a qualifying class: a single paying shared layer (or, under the experience identification, a single conscious whole) is maintained across a cut that violates a pre-registered LAT-1 budget. **UNTESTED.**

**C-IIT-4. Group lifetime still scales as \(\sqrt{k}\).** (Already a dated prediction, 21 November 2026, on `theory/FLUID_LAYERS_2026-11-21.md`; **not** a new consciousness claim.) Median lifetime of a shared layer scales as \(\sqrt{k}\) at fixed drift of who should be grouped (CL19). IIT has no redesign-cost band and so does not predict that exponent for the lifetime of a complex. A miss is already stated there: a measured log-log slope of lifetime on re-forming cost outside a preregistered band around \(1/2\), or lifetimes that do not move when \(k\) moves by more than \(10\times\). **OPEN**, not locked, as that page already says.

**Weak-programme candidate, not one of C-IIT-1–4 and also not preregistered.** At fixed wiring, PCI (or another practical \(\Phi\)-like index that is not itself used as the definition of consciousness) should move with stakes if it tracks \(I_J\) or the stay line, because those costs depend on \(w\); IIT's \(\varphi_s\) is stake-free, so a stake-free intrinsic measure on unchanged connectivity is not predicted to move with \(w\). A miss stays open for the mapping of that index onto the cost, not a miss of C-IIT-1–4, and it does not require the experience identification.

## 6. Microtubules and Orch-OR: SPECULATION, and not implied

Orch-OR is a different proposal, not a corollary of the cost or of the swarm rule.

**The proposal (SPECULATION as an account of experience).** Hameroff and Penrose 2014 identify conscious moments with orchestrated objective reductions of quantum superpositions in microtubules, the objective-reduction step being the gravitational collapse proposed by Penrose.

**What is actually tested, and what is not.** Microtubules are real cytoskeletal polymers; that is not evidence of quantum computation, and not evidence of experience. Tegmark 2000 argued that relevant decoherence times are far shorter than neural timescales; that is a **KNOWN** calculation, later contested in modeling, not a measurement of decoherence in living neurons. Reimers, McKemmish, McKenzie, Mark and Hush 2014 judged the revised proposal "not scientifically justified"; this page takes that as a serious, citable critique, not as the last word of nature. Babcock, Montes-Cabrera, Oberhofer, Chergui, Celardo and Kurian 2024 report modeled tryptophan collectivity and an in-vitro fluorescence-yield increase in microtubules versus tubulin, not gravitational objective reduction and not experience. Citing that paper as support for Orch-OR would be an overreach.

Minimizing the cost of judgment does not produce a Diósi–Penrose collapse, and does not need one. If later measurements were to show functionally relevant quantum coherence in tubulin, that would be a fact about the *hardware* of a store or a clock. It would still have to pay the same cost test: does the coherence buy prediction worth its keep? That is CT9 again, not a new consciousness theorem.
