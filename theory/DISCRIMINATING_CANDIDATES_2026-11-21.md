### 1. Dipteran early vision: priced cutoff or count versus whitening, in a measured near-1/f intensity world

**System and qualification.** Photoreceptors and lamina monopolar cells (LMCs) of Diptera, tested against published light-intensity time series. The series used below are not fly-in-habitat recordings. Van Hateren 1997 measured intensity with an optical device "worn on a headband by a freely walking person," with a field of view "comparable with … single human foveal cones," then "presented to the visual system of the blowfly (*Calliphora vicina*) by playing them back … on a [LED]." The paper says fly-in-habitat series would be ideal and were not obtained with sufficient accuracy, and that the human series are only "expected to be roughly equivalent" (with listed differences in acceptance angle, speed, and object distance). The mismatch is three-way: human-walking world, *Calliphora* responses, *D. melanogaster* N_e. Calling this "flying Diptera, acting in natural light-intensity time series" overstates what was measured. This weakens the candidate as a *Drosophila* field class; a miss in a class not shown to qualify is not a field kill, and the human world source is a listed limitation, not silently ignored.

Qualification prospect: *D. melanogaster* has a sourced species-wide N_e = 8.6 × 10^5 (Sung et al. 2016, Table 1, fetched). That figure is the Sung Table 1 default, replaced if a sourced visual-lineage or species-wide estimate is smaller. A smaller N_e *widens* ε = 10/(σ·N_e), which is conservative for a kill and anti-conservative for a pass; the word "conservative" is not used here without that direction. T_red itself is not sourced, and no cost-to-fitness map σ for photoreceptor or LMC design was found or fetched. So the class is **not shown to qualify**. A result on this system can test the *filter/layer prediction* in a plausibly selected organ; it becomes a test of the hypothesis only after (a)–(e) are recorded. SN1 already recorded that in *Drosophila*, photon shot noise is exogenous (Model S is the right noise class; the store-noise SN1 arm is INVALID there). The Juusola & Hardie 2001 JGP full text was not retrieved this session (publisher and PMC PDF URLs returned 403 or an HTML stub). The 50 → 10 ms bump-duration figure lives in `theory/measured/SN1_RESULTS.md` and is not re-extracted here.

**GTAO prediction (primary: CT21 / N60, priced cutoff or count versus whitening; CT12 only if a discrete read is defined and α is in range).** The world input is the measured temporal spectrum of the published intensity series, plus photon noise. For a scale-free continuum, K\* and the frequency cutoff are set by stakes, sensing noise and upkeep h, not by a single world time scale (CORE.md §4; THEORY §2). THEORY L3 / CT21 (THEORY line 81): "A continuum of time scales has no exact finite design (L3)." A near-1/f world may not furnish a finite K\* at all. Interlacing (L2) applies only at a full-rank Model S optimum with separated real modes, which a 1/f continuum may not provide.

CT12's 1/α elasticity (THEORY B.3, the setting and **CT12**, about lines 335–339) is for *exact reset reads* that hold the last value, with structure function E(X_{t+τ} − X_t)² = q·τ^{α−1} on the open interval **1 < α < 3**. The optimal interval is T\* = [α·c/((α − 1)·w·q)]^{1/α}. That is not the theorem class for continuous dipteran photoreceptors and LMCs, which are analogue filters, not exact reset reads. The closer theorem for continuous sensing is Lemma B2 (THEORY B.3): with white observation noise of density σ_n², memory time ∝ (σ_n²/q)^{1/α}, and precision effort with a linear price ∝ w^{α/(2α−1)} q^{1/(2α−1)}. At α = 2 that stakes elasticity is 2/3, not CT12's 1/α = 1/2 and not R2's 1/2. A VIS-1F elasticity arm must say which theorem is in force, and must not default to 1/α unless a discrete exact-reset read is operationalized.

**γ → α map (written before any fit).** For a process with temporal PSD ∝ 1/f^γ, the structure function scales as E(ΔX)² ∝ τ^{γ−1}, with a logarithmic special case at γ = 1. Mapping onto CT12's E(ΔX)² = q·τ^{α−1} gives **α = γ**. Van Hateren 1997 has "γ here close to 1." That likely puts α at or outside the proved open interval 1 < α < 3 (at α = 1 the error term becomes age-independent, and (α − 1) in the denominator of T\* is zero). The CT12 1/α arm is therefore **not in scope** for this candidate unless a later Pass 1 fit, under this map and the pre-written error band, shows α strictly inside (1, 3) *and* a discrete exact-reset read is defined. Numerical checks of T\* in `theory/MASTER_EQUATION.md` start at α = 1.3; that is not the proved interval.

The distinctive GTAO content, if any, is a *priced* deviation of cutoff or layer count from the SNR-whitening design, not CT12 and not a default finite K\*. Filter *shape* agreeing with whitening is a collapse into efficient coding, not a pass for the hypothesis.

**Named rivals.** (R3) design equals the world's time scales — ill-posed if the world has no characteristic time. (R2) every interval scales with exponent ½. (Efficient coding / whitening) already named, Atick & Redlich 1990, *Neural Computation*, doi 10.1162/neco.1990.2.3.308 (Crossref OK, issued 1990; works record verified this revision; full text not fetched): filters from the natural spectrum and SNR, including temporal whitening. The 1997 paper sits on that line and on Laughlin 1981, *Z. Naturforsch. C*, doi 10.1515/znc-1981-9-1040 (Crossref OK, issued 1981; static contrast coding); Atick & Redlich 1992, *Neural Computation*, doi 10.1162/neco.1992.4.2.196 (Crossref OK, issued 1992; "What Does the Retina Know about Natural Scenes?"); and van Hateren 1992a, *J. Comp. Physiol. A*, doi 10.1007/bf00188924 (Crossref OK, issued 1992; "Theoretical predictions of spatiotemporal receptive fields of fly LMCs, and experimental validation"). Van Hateren 1997 already reports that LMC spectra become almost flat while photoreceptor spectra still follow 1/f. (Fairhall, Lewen, Bialek & de Ruyter van Steveninck 2001; Wark, Fairhall & Rieke 2009): scale-free adaptation, already scored in BIO1 as a slope ≈ 1 that does not separate tuning from scale-free dynamics.

**Fetched world facts (van Hateren 1997, *Vision Res.*, doi 10.1016/S0042-6989(97)00105-3, Crossref OK; author PDF from the University of Groningen, 1,201,248 bytes, fetched in the shortlist session).** Intensity time series recorded from a freely walking human and played back to *Calliphora vicina*, as disclosed above; low-pass limited to 100 Hz; power spectra from 20.48 s sections of a 45-minute stretch "behave approximately as 1/f^γ … γ here close to 1." At frequencies lower than 0.1–1 Hz the spectra "deviate somewhat from a strict power-law behaviour." "Part of this adaptation is very fast, with a significant change in gain within a few tens of milliseconds." LMC spectra "do not follow the 1/f behaviour: these spectra become almost flat" under wide-field illumination (whitening).

**Separation, quantified as far as this session allows, and no further.** This is **not** a locked gap. A 1/f continuum has no single τ_world, so R3 is not scorable without first fitting a corner. If the low-frequency deviation below 0.1–1 Hz were treated as a world mode (1–10 s) and the observed gain adaptation is "a few tens of milliseconds," the ratio would be on the order of 30–300, which is larger than the theory's own 7–15× worked example (`theory/MASTER_EQUATION.md` §1a / `theory/meq_checks/meq_roots.out`; the §2 point estimate in the same hypothetical example is 1.5 and 6.9 days). That reading is tempting and is **not** used as a number: the paper presents a near-1/f law with a mild low-frequency deviation, not a fitted real pole, and the adaptation phrase is not a filter-pole estimate. Against R2, a 1/α elasticity is not advertised for this system (wrong theorem class; γ close to 1 likely puts α at or outside 1 < α < 3). Against efficient coding, **no numerical gap was computed**. Filter *shape* is expected to collapse into whitening (Laughlin / Atick / van Hateren) unless a priced upkeep h, with (h, w) sourced independently of the design, moves the cutoff, or the layer *count*, away from the SNR-whitening design by more than the error band. Until that is computed, candidate 1 is a hope, not a shown discriminator. That computation is Pass 1 of the draft prereg, under the concrete gate in §5. If it fails, or if (h, w) cannot be sourced independently, or if L3 leaves both K\* and a priced cutoff undefined, this candidate is not locked.

**Public data fetched (this session only).** van Hateren 1997 author PDF; Sung 2016 Table 1 (N_e); Crossref records below, including the efficient-coding papers credited above (works records only). Juusola & Hardie full text not fetched.

**Cost and time.** Pass 1 (fit γ and any corners from the published spectra; apply the γ → α map; compute a priced K\* or cutoff versus a whitening cutoff, and versus any scorable R3 corner, only if (h, w) can be sourced independently): about one to three days of analysis, no new experiment, no account. ### 2. E. coli, a price-dependence arm that is not τ\* versus τ_v

**System and qualification.** The same substrate class as the locked E. coli plan: motility / chemotaxis as a judgment design in *E. coli*. (b) N_e is ESTABLISHED (1.7960 × 10^8). (a), (c), (d), (e) remain NOT ESTABLISHED. Honda SI Fig. S2E and Ni Datasets S1–S4 were again not retrieved (HTTP 403). This is still the best qualification *prospect*, not a qualified class.

**GTAO prediction (N60 / CT21 / CT33, not the locked Arm B memory).** A layer is paid for only if R(ε_{k−1} − ε_k) > 1, with R = w E_∞ / h. Judgment spend (receptors, Che proteins, memory), not flagellar actuation as such, should track the stake of being wrong, and total spend should rise with stakes while the number of layers may fall if reads replace stores (CT33). This is not a re-score of P1, P3, P4 or P13, and it is not Tjalma's 1/g adaptation-time prediction.

**Named rivals, all already in the literature on this system.** (R3) no price dependence: same design whenever the physical gradient timescales are the same. (R1 / Scott, Gunderson, Mateescu, Zhang & Hwa 2010, *Science*, doi 10.1126/science.1192588, Crossref OK; full text not fetched, abstract only): proteome sectors track growth rate, not chemotaxis benefit as such. (Ni, Colin, Link, Endres & Sourjik 2020, *PNAS*, doi 10.1073/pnas.1910849117, Crossref issued 2019; Europe PMC XML `PMC6955288` fetched): "investment in motility in proportion to the reproductive fitness advantage provided by the ability to follow nutrient gradients," including a model with growth-dependent proteome partitioning; this regulation "occurs even when nutrient gradients are absent" (anticipatory preallocation). WT cells grew "almost twice as fast" as Δ*cheY* on ribose; "no significant difference" on glucose; that WT (chemotaxis) advantage vanished when the culture was stirred; "the cost of the chemotactic signaling, incurred by the CheY phosphorylation cycle, is negligible compared to the overall cellular energy budget." (Honda, Cremer, Mancini, Zhang, Pilizota & Hwa 2022, doi 10.1073/pnas.2110342119; Europe PMC XML and bioRxiv PDF 10.1101/2021.05.12.443892 fetched): motility gene expression rises at slow growth (~4.4-fold over the growth-rate range, in the published 2022 XML and in the preprint) "to compensate for reduction in cell size" so that flagella per cell stay "nearly independent of growth rate"; Δ*flhD* "increased growth rate by up to 18%." (Lisevich, Colin, Yang, Ni & Sourjik 2025, *Nat. Commun.*, doi 10.1038/s41467-025-56980-x, PDF fetched): swimming velocity saturates with flagellar number; wild-type expression in rich medium sits near that saturation; investment in minimal media still tracks carbon quality / benefit. (Tjalma & ten Wolde 2024): adaptation time, already the rival for the locked timing arm.

**Separation.** **Not shown, and at high risk of collapse.** Ni 2020 already states the qualitative "investment follows potential benefit" prediction. Honda 2022 already states a different quantitative rule (constant flagella per cell). A GTAO arm is distinctive only if it predicts a *judgment* quantity (signaling proteome, layer count, memory) that those two curves do not, in a condition that breaks the confound between growth rate and chemotaxis benefit (the usual poor-carbon / glucose contrast is both slower growth and higher potential benefit). Ni already found that expression still tracks growth rate when gradients are absent, which is a problem for a static test that uses the *current* lab stake w: current w ≈ 0 in a well-mixed culture, yet the design is not off. That is either a miss for static argmin J, or it forces the "world" to be the natural one and the lab expression to be a cue — in which case the lab well-mixed condition is not a test. I did not fetch a proteome table that separates Che/Tar from flagella across conditions (Schmidt et al. 2016, doi 10.1038/nbt.3418, Crossref OK; PRIDE PXD000498 exists, raw files not downloaded). Without that, or a sourced N60 threshold, no numerical gap is claimed.

### 3. CT12 elasticity 1/α versus ½, in a system that can qualify, with α ≠ 2

**System and qualification.** The distinctive content of CT12 against R2 exists only when the world's spectral exponent is not 2 (TESTBEDS already noted that at α = 2 the √ law is prior art: Barro 1972, Dixit 1991; also Senders 1964, Sims 2003), and only for exact reset reads (THEORY B.3), not for continuous filters (Lemma B2). N51 is this test, written for an online lab learner, and does not qualify. A qualifying version needs a class from the Sung table (or a sourced equivalent) with a measured α ≠ 2 strictly inside (1, 3), a measured exact-reset read or update rate, and sourced σ and T_red.

**GTAO prediction (CT12, CT13).** With exact reset reads, T\* ∝ (c/w)^{1/α} q^{-1/α} (equivalently σ^{-2/α} in the usual scaling), so elasticities are 1/α and −1/α, not ½.

**Named rivals.** R2 (always ½). Mora & Nemenman 2019, *Phys. Rev. Lett.*, doi 10.1103/PhysRevLett.123.198101 (Crossref OK; arXiv PDF 1908.04057 fetched): the world is taken to be a geometric random walk with characteristic time τ (the α = 2 case); they derive a bound δc/c ∼ (Dacτ)^{-1/4}, "in stark contrast with the usual Berg and Purcell bound δc/c ∼ (DacT)^{-1/2}," and a network that "adapts the kinetics of signaling as a function of the square root of the sensed concentration." The repository already recorded that the α = 2 case is in this paper ([`history/2026-11-20_queue/QUEUE.md`](https://github.com/Chad-Mitchell/theory-of-adaptive-organization/blob/1eed183f3888e8e3eead5956bbf1e8cb4233510a/process/QUEUE.md), PRIORART-LFK-ALPHA). R1 (fixed budget share) makes no α-dependent elasticity. R3 has no price dependence.

**Separation.** At a locked α = 1.5 or 2.5, 1/α is 0.667 or 0.400, versus R2's 0.500. N51 already treats that contrast as the kill. **No qualifying public dataset with α, rates, and prices was fetched in this session.** Yeast Hog1 (Zi, Liebermeister & Klipp 2010, *PLoS One*, doi 10.1371/journal.pone.0009522, PDF fetched; SN1 system 3) has laboratory pulse periods (activation time "about 1 min"; periodic square pulses) and no measured natural osmotic spectrum, so λ is still missing, as SN1 already said. Candidate 1's near-1/f world is the closest fetched α ≠ 2 spectrum, and under the γ → α map (α = γ) van Hateren's "γ here close to 1" likely puts it at or outside CT12's proved range, which is one reason the CT12 arm is not the primary arm of candidate 1.

**Cost and time.** Designing and running N51 remains the cheapest *clean* exponent test (already in OPEN_WORK as N51-LAB-PREREG), but it does not test the hypothesis. A qualifying microbial or insect version, without a fetched dataset, is a lab or a new data search measured in weeks, not a one-day analysis.

### 4. Social-insect sharing threshold N\*(ρ) versus Beekman / Dornhaus

**System and qualification.** Trail or dance recruitment as a shared judgment layer. FRAMEWORK already rates colony-level selection as moderate and lists ants as needing experiments. No colony-level N_e, σ, or T_red was fetched. Pharaoh's ants and honey bees are not in the Sung table. **Qualification prospect is weak.**

**GTAO prediction (CT14 for the onset; CT16 for the telling price; both in Result 4).** A shared layer pays iff N > N\*(ρ) = (√(1+ρ)+1)²/ρ, which is CT14 at the square-root technology (a = b = ½). Telling pays under the stated d/c rule (CT16).

**Named rivals.** Beekman, Sumpter & Ratnieks 2001, *PNAS*, doi 10.1073/pnas.161285298 (Crossref OK). A full text was not re-opened in the independent review: the PMC HTML is a reCAPTCHA wall, and Europe PMC XML returned HTTP 500. The Crossref abstract supports a first-order, hysteretic transition from disordered to organized foraging in small versus larger colonies, but does not contain the numbers "600" or "0.0052." Those two numbers were quoted in the first version of this note from the 2001 paper ("colonies of 600 ants or fewer cannot form a trail toward a feeder, whereas larger colonies can"; independent discovery rate α = 0.0052 per ant per min). They are **not re-verified** in this revision and are not used as if they had been re-checked. Their own positive-feedback trail model already predicts a nonlinear threshold. Dornhaus, Klügl, Oechslein, Puppe & Chittka 2006, *Behav. Ecol.*, doi 10.1093/beheco/arj036 (Crossref OK). The author PDF was not re-opened in the independent review (OUP article-PDF 403; Unpaywall not OA). The first version of this note quoted "Colony size, a factor often suspected to influence recruitment evolution, had no significant effect"; that sentence is also **not re-verified** here. BIO1 already: T4 (√N trail fade) untested; no data linking evaporation to colony size with independent depositors.

**Separation.** **Not quantified.** GTAO's N\*(ρ) is typically O(1–20) for moderate ρ (e.g. ρ = 1 gives N\* = (√2+1)² ≈ 5.8). Comparing that onset to a worker-count at trail onset, or treating a discovery rate as ρ, would be a category error unless a preregistered, sourced map is written first. The numbers 600 and 0.0052 are not used for that arithmetic in this revision, because they have not been re-verified against a full text. Even with a map, Beekman's model already predicts *a* threshold, so existence of a threshold is not distinctive; only a different *number* or a different dependence on ρ would be.

**Cost and time.** Re-reading Beekman, once a full text can be opened again, and writing a mapping: a day, and likely a no-go at the separation or mapping gate. New trail experiments: a lab or field collaborator, not public data.

### 5. Firm or inventory forecasting: interlacing versus "set at," on public demand series
