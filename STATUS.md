## Where the theory stands

**The hypothesis.** The law of adaptive organization (hypothesis): strongly selected systems minimize the cost of judgment. It is untested (`THEORY.md:41`). No real system has been shown to qualify under `THEORY.md:45-51`.

**Two cost objects, not one.** The repository keeps them apart (`CORE.md:15, 25-39`).

1. **The master equation** (`THEORY.md:26-40`): \(J=\kappa I_{\mathrm{mem}}+hK+w\,\mathbb{E}[\ell]+A+L\) subject to \(P(\mathrm{ruin})\le\varepsilon_r\). Holding memory (\(\kappa I_{\mathrm{mem}}\), the price per nat held, plus \(hK\), the upkeep per layer), being wrong or late (\(w\,\mathbb{E}[\ell]\)), and acquiring and sharing information (\(A+L\)). It is a stated definition; its term list, linear prices, and ruin cap are assumptions. The layer-count, lifetime, and local-versus-shared theorems in `THEORY.md` are theorems about this sum.
2. **The physical cost** in proved classes (`CORE.md:25-27, 70`): \(J_{\mathrm{phys}}=\dot Q+w\,\mathbb{E}[\ell]\) subject to the ruin cap, the bath heat of the memory plus the stake on loss. Its thermodynamic floor is \(kT\) times the net learning rate, **KNOWN** for bipartite Markov chains and **PROVED (in-model)** for additive-noise diffusions (`CORE.md:70`, Horowitz and Esposito 2014). Class by class on `main`:
   - *Exogenous equilibrium and telegraph classes* (`CORE.md:86-94`, `theory/GENERAL_FORM_PHYSICS_2026-11-22.md`): equilibrium Ornstein–Uhlenbeck signals, independent modes, the two-state telegraph \(kT\,r\,L(\varepsilon)\), the finite-state bound (one direction only), the drift-spring factor, and the open frozen-or-disconnected boundary.
   - *Non-Gaussian, regime-switching, and reset-shock classes* (T49, `theory/NON_GAUSSIAN_FLOOR_2026-11-23.md:13-24`): motion sets the floor's shape (\(1/\varepsilon\) for diffusive motion, \(\ln(1/\varepsilon)\) for reset shocks); tail shape enters only as a bounded credit, and shock tracking selects a "core plus reserve" memory (\(\delta=\varepsilon/4v\)) rather than light-tailed commitment.
   - *Feedback boundary* (T61, `theory/FEEDBACK_FLOOR_2026-11-23.md:13-35`; independent review reported in OPEN_WORK; no review record committed). T61 is heat accounting for a passive plant. It is not co-adaptation F3/F4. \(\dot Q_M\ge kT\,I_{\mathrm{alt}}\) with \(I_{\mathrm{alt}}=-I^X\) still holds when the memory steers the world, while T60's exogenous bare-rate floor fails (acting can lower or reverse \(I_{\mathrm{alt}}\)); conservative traps have vanishing ongoing heat, whereas additive linear feedback separates compensated Kalman–Bucy control from uncompensated loops.
   - *Clock term and Kalman–Bucy factor of two* (T68, `theory/CLOCK_TERM_2026-11-23.md:11-15, 125`; `theory/DRIFT_HEAT_LIFT_2026-11-23.md:128-129`): anticipating predictable change between paid looks needs a TUR-priced internal clock once looks cost more than \(4\kappa kT\) or \(6\kappa kT\) (fixed price) or carry more than \(4\kappa/\lambda\) nats (information-priced random looks); the Kalman–Bucy factor-of-two question is answered from the linear identity (\(\dot Q_M=kT\,\dot I_M(1+\dot I_M/N)\)) and Hartich–Barato–Seifert 2016 (with its transfer-entropy qualification).
   - *Record-repair floor and cooperative loophole* (T63, `theory/RECORD_REPAIR_FLOOR_2026-11-23.md:11-21, 114-116, 174`): keeping a vanished bit in leaky carriers costs at least \(4kT\,\Gamma(\ln 2-h(\varepsilon))-kT\,S_{\max}/\tau\) (generally \(kT\,\rho(\ln 2-h)\)), bounded under repetition coding; 2D/3D classical cooperative media (and 4D quantum, with 3D quantum open) evade the linear-in-volume floor via exponential spectral-gap decay.

IP5, the claim that the master equation is the physical frontier, is FALSE (`measurement/CLAIM_MAP_v2.md` section Y). Putting the physical display into `THEORY.md` §3 is open work T40. Until a reviewed proposal is adopted, `THEORY.md` §3 prints the five-term sum and governs (`CORE.md:15`).
