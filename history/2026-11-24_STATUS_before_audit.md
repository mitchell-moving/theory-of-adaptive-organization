## Where the theory stands

**The hypothesis.** The law of adaptive organization (hypothesis): strongly selected systems minimize the cost of judgment. It is untested. No system has been shown to qualify under THEORY §3.

**Two cost objects, not one.** They are different, and the repository keeps them apart.

1. **The master equation** (THEORY §3): \(J=\kappa I_{\mathrm{mem}}+hK+w\,\mathbb{E}[\ell]+A+L\) subject to \(P(\mathrm{ruin})\le\varepsilon_r\). Holding memory (\(\kappa I_{\mathrm{mem}}\), the price per nat held, plus \(hK\), the upkeep per layer), being wrong or late (\(w\,\mathbb{E}[\ell]\)), and acquiring and sharing information (\(A+L\)). It is a stated definition. Its term list, its linear prices, and its ruin cap are assumptions. The layer-count, lifetime, and local-versus-shared theorems in THEORY are theorems about this sum.
2. **The physical cost** in the proved class: \(J_{\mathrm{phys}}=\dot Q+w\,\mathbb{E}[\ell]\) subject to the ruin cap, the heat of the memory's bath plus the stake on the loss. Its thermodynamic heat floor is \(kT\) times the net learning rate, **KNOWN** for bipartite Markov chains and **PROVED (in-model)** for additive-noise diffusions (Horowitz and Esposito 2014). The class-by-class informational floor — the telegraph \(kT\,r\,L(\varepsilon)\), the connected finite-state bound (one direction only), the drift-spring factor, and the still-open frozen or disconnected boundary — is the statement in `CORE.md` §3. The worked minimizer class: an exogenous equilibrium Ornstein–Uhlenbeck signal, independent signals of that kind, or the two-state telegraph; mean-square or Hamming loss; a memory that does not move the signal (`theory/GENERAL_FORM_PHYSICS_2026-11-22.md`).

IP5, the claim that the master equation is the physical frontier, is FALSE (CLAIM_MAP section Y). Putting the physical display into THEORY §3 is open work T40. Until a reviewed proposal is adopted, THEORY §3 prints the five-term sum, and that file governs.

**What the axioms give.** Theorem R1 (`theory/REPRESENTATION.md`, CLAIM_MAP V1) assumes seven axioms, A1–A7. On at least three essential interval coordinates, a continuous cost that represents the preorder is an additive priced sum, up to one positive scale and one additive constant. R1 does not choose the coordinate list. A6 fixes only which information is the memory coordinate: finite-law Shannon mutual information on finite alphabets, and, when the mutual information is finite, the Gelfand–Yaglom–Dobrushin supremum (`theory/CONTINUOUS_A6_2026-11-23.md`). That the coordinates are memory, loss, acquisition and links is an assumption, and which pricing of \(A\) applies is open (V4). Given the list, R1 does not sign the prices: nonnegative prices are an eighth assumption, A8. It does not cover the discrete layer count: \(hK\) enters only under an extra separability assumption. So the five-term display is R1's form on an assumed list, with A8 and that extra assumption. It is not derived from the axioms alone. The four demands "store, predict, act, stay solvent" do not force the equation (R2). The same letters A1–A3 in `theory/SELECTION_AXIOMS.md`, and A1–A5 in THEORY Appendix B, are different axioms.

**Proved, in named classes.** The heat floor and its minimizers in the class above (one OU signal, the stake threshold, the geometric-ladder layer count, the rank-one memory for a sum of modes, the telegraph). Five yields of the minimum, each in its own class: layers (THEORY), holographic memory (T42, T41), the boundary (T44), rebuild under the ruin cap (T43), and acting (T45, T46). The acting derivation (T47) is merged and not adopted. That the five yields are one minimum is a synthesis (`theory/ACTING_LOOP_2026-11-22.md` §8), not one theorem.
